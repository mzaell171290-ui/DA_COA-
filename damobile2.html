FAQs

How can we help you?

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach the COA Public Information Office through the contact details in the footer.

What is the "Disaster Audit" site by the Commission on Audit, and what should I know about its purpose?

The Disaster Audit site is an official Commission on Audit (COA) portal that publishes audit findings, summaries, and related documentation on disaster fund utilization.

Who should I contact for media, research, or official inquiries?

Reach the COA Public Information Office through the email, phone number, and address listed in the footer below.

How is personal or sensitive information handled on the site?

The site publishes only official audit findings and fund allocation records — it does not collect or display personal data about individual beneficiaries.

How do I request additional information or report a records request?

Send a request to the Public Information Office by email or phone, listed in the footer, and include the specific report or region you're asking about.
